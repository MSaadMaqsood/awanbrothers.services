Import Logistics

When your cargo lands at Karachi Port or Port Qasim, we take it from there.

We coordinate with your clearing agent, arrange the right vehicle for the release, and deliver to your warehouse, factory or site anywhere in Pakistan. You can follow the load in our TMS from port gate to your door.

  • Pickups from Karachi Port & Port Qasim
  • Coordination with your clearing agent
  • Container & loose cargo delivery
  • Empty container return to yard
  • Delivery anywhere in Pakistan
  • Live tracking, port to door

We plan export movements backwards from your sailing date: empty container pickup, stuffing at your factory, and gate-in at the port on time, with every step visible in our TMS.

  • Empty container pickup & positioning
  • Factory stuffing supervision
  • Timed gate-in for vessel cut-offs
  • Coordination with forwarders & lines
  • Up-country loads to Karachi ports
  • Digital documents & status reports
