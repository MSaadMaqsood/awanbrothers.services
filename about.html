Who we are

Brothers in business, partners on the road

Awan Brothers Services started with a simple idea: treat every customer's cargo as if it were our own.

Today we are a carriage contractor and supplier of heavy-duty vehicles, earth-moving equipment and labour, working from the heart of Karachi's industrial belt. Being next to Port Qasim and on the National Highway means we are where the cargo is.

What sets us apart is our own Transport Management System. It gives our team and our clients the same clear view of every shipment, from booking to delivery.
