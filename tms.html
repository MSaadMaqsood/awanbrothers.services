FAQ

Questions about our TMS

Do I need to install anything?

No. The client portal works in any web browser on your phone or computer. Tracking links can also be shared with you by SMS or WhatsApp.

How do I get access to the client portal?

Portal access is set up for clients with active shipments. Contact our team and we'll create logins for you and your colleagues.

How often is the vehicle location updated?

Vehicles report their GPS position regularly throughout the trip, and milestone events such as loading, checkpoints and delivery are logged as they happen.

Can I get reports on my company's shipments?

Yes. We can share regular reports on volumes, transit times and delivery performance for your routes.

Is my shipment data kept private?

Yes. Each client only sees their own shipments, documents and reports.
